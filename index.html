About Us

Bush dancing is a traditional style of folk dancing accompanied by an acoustic band which blends vibrant British, Irish, and European themes. While having been present in Australia from the earliest days, today in Sydney we work to keep this scene alive by hosting shindigs celebrating timeless Aussie tradition!

Join us one evening to experience magic that moved Australians through the generations! We have a talented team of performers who bring the energy, and infectious spirit of traditional bush dancing to life. Whether you're a seasoned dancer or it's your first time, there's something for everyone!
