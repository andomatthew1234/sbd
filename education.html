Connect with Culture

We offer a wide variety of educational workshops tailored to schools. Our workshops are a fun and inclusive way to engage students of all ages.

A Bush Dance is a unique element of Australian Cultural heritage. Our 1 hour workshops equip students with basic dance steps, and enable them to connect with Australia’s historic roots.

Bush Dancing has been shown to promote uniquely beneficial forms of socialisation due to its blend of group dance and live music. It’s also an excellent way to keep students active!
